# FGCT6021 reflective report template

**Maximum length:** 1,200 words. This file is a scaffold, not a completed report. Replace every bracketed prompt with your own evidence and reflection, then remove the prompts. Do not invent participant feedback, test outcomes, or personal learning. Check the module's word-count rules and cite sources in the format your tutor requires.

Suggested allocation totals 1,100 words, leaving a small margin for editing. Keep references and captions within the limit if the module guidance says they count.

## Project and user need — about 150 words

[Briefly explain the app concept, intended user, and need. State which parts are supported by your own research and which remain assumptions. Give the date and method of any student conversations, how many people took part, how consent and anonymity were handled, and the main finding. If research is not complete, say so plainly.]

## How workshops informed the app — about 180 words

[Describe specific techniques from the taught workshop code that you applied. For example, explain a relevant MAUI layout/navigation exercise, the Week 4 API/JSON work, and database concepts only where those weeks were actually covered and evidenced in your work. Refer to a screenshot, code location, or development log for each example. Avoid listing weeks without explaining how the learning changed the app.]

## Mini-project extension and implementation — about 200 words

[Explain which features extend the workshop exercises and why you chose them. Describe the mobile/backend relationship, SQLite demo ledger, API use, and the assistant or external sandbox feature that you actually completed. Be precise: StudentBank internal transfers affect fictional coursework data; TrueLayer is sandbox/read-only; the assistant does not make financial decisions or move money.]

## Technical decisions and challenges — about 220 words

[Discuss the most important implementation problem you personally encountered, the alternatives considered, what you changed, and the evidence that the change worked. Include a real build/run issue or design trade-off, not a generic claim. Mention limitations that remain, such as server availability, local SQLite, sandbox access, or unverified platform behavior.]

## Evaluation and iteration — about 250 words

[Describe the actual evaluation method and findings. Include participant task results or peer critique only if those sessions took place; use anonymized codes and concise evidence. State what design/code change you made in response and how you checked it. If no session has happened yet, do not substitute this repository's desk review: report the evaluation as incomplete and explain the next step.]

## Strengths, limitations, and learning — about 100 words

[Reflect in your own words on what works, what does not, accessibility/privacy/security concerns, and what you learned. Explain what you would change with more time. Do not describe the prototype as production banking or claim full offline support: the offline cache is read-only and limited to saved demo-ledger data.]
